HabitSpark AI Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App"). The App is a habit-tracking tool powered by AI to provide personalized suggestions and features like adding friends via Facebook integration. By using the App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with these terms, please do not access or use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways, including:

Personal Data

  • User Authentication and Profile Information: When you register or log in, we collect your email address, name, and profile picture from Firebase Authentication. If you use Facebook login for adding friends, we collect your Facebook user ID, friends list (with your permission), and basic profile details (name, email, profile picture) as provided by Facebook.
  • Habit Data: Information about your habits, such as habit names, progress, streaks, and completion status, which you input into the App.

Derived Data

  • AI Suggestions: Data generated from your habits to provide personalized AI recommendations, including usage patterns and preferences.
  • Device and Usage Information: Automatically collected data such as device type, operating system, IP address, app version, and usage statistics (for example, time spent in the App and features used) to improve the App.

Location Data

We do not collect location data unless you enable optional features requiring it (for example, location-based habits), in which case we collect approximate location with your consent.

Data from Third Parties

  • From Facebook: Friends data for social features, only if you authorize the integration.
  • From Firebase: Analytics data for app performance.

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as financial data, biometric data, or health data beyond self-reported habit information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain core App features, including habit tracking, AI-powered suggestions, and friend additions via Facebook.
  • To send notifications (if enabled), such as habit reminders or friend updates.
  • To personalize your experience, including tailoring AI suggestions based on your habits.
  • To analyze app usage for improvements and bug fixes.
  • To comply with legal obligations and respond to user requests (for example, data deletion).
  • For security purposes, including detecting and preventing fraud or unauthorized access.

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Sharing Your Information

We may share your information only in limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With vendors such as Firebase (authentication and database) and Facebook (friend integration). These providers are contractually required to protect your data and use it only to provide services to us.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, such as in response to a court order or government request.
  • Business Transfers: If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred.
  • With Your Consent: For any other purpose with your explicit permission.

We do not share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Facebook/Meta Integration and Data Handling

If you choose to connect your Facebook account to add friends:

  • We request permissions for your public profile, email, and friends list solely to enable social features in the App (for example, discovering and adding friends who use HabitSpark AI).
  • Data from Facebook is used only for the intended purpose and is not stored longer than necessary.
  • We comply with Meta's Platform Terms and Developer Policies, including limited data use and secure handling.
  • You can revoke Facebook permissions at any time via your Facebook settings or the App's account management, which stops future data access.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy:

  • Account and habit data: Retained while your account is active and for up to 30 days after deletion to allow recovery.
  • Usage analytics: Anonymized and retained for up to 2 years for app improvement.
  • Facebook data: Stored temporarily (up to 7 days) for friend features and deleted upon revocation or account deletion.

Backups may retain data for up to 90 days for recovery purposes.

6. Security of Your Information

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information, including encryption for data in transit and at rest via Firebase. However, no transmission method over the Internet or electronic storage method is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable data protection laws, including GDPR (EU) and CCPA/CPRA (California), such as:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations).
  • Opt-Out: Opt out of certain processing activities.
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@habitspark.ai. We will respond within applicable legal timelines.

For CCPA: We do not sell personal information. Categories collected in the past 12 months may include identifiers (email, name), user-provided habit information, and internet activity (usage statistics), disclosed to service providers for business purposes.

For GDPR: We process data based on consent (for Facebook integration and notifications) and legitimate interests (for core app functionality). You may withdraw consent at any time via App settings.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or 16 in some jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we discover such data, we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you by posting the updated policy in the App or via email. Continued use of the App after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
